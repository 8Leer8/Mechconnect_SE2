                              ABOUT MECHCONNECT
================================================================================

What is MechConnect?

MechConnect is your trusted on-demand automotive repair and maintenance platform 
that connects vehicle owners with skilled, verified mechanics and auto repair 
shops in your area. Whether you need an emergency roadside repair, routine 
maintenance, or specialized automotive services, MechConnect brings professional 
help directly to you.

Our Mission

We aim to revolutionize the automotive service industry by making vehicle 
repairs more accessible, transparent, and convenient. Our platform empowers 
both vehicle owners and automotive professionals by creating a reliable 
marketplace built on trust and quality service.

How It Works

FOR CLIENTS:
- Browse available mechanics and shops in your area
- Request services through Direct, Custom, Emergency, or Broadcast modes
- Get real-time pricing and service estimates
- Track your mechanic's arrival and service progress
- Pay securely through the app
- Rate and review service providers

FOR MECHANICS:
- Register and verify your credentials
- Set your own service prices and availability
- Receive service requests from nearby clients
- Navigate to client locations with built-in maps
- Build your reputation through customer reviews
- Grow your business and income

FOR SHOP OWNERS:
- List your shop services and specialties
- Manage your team of mechanics
- Accept service bookings from customers
- Showcase your business to a wider audience
- Maintain verified shop credentials

Key Features

✓ Multiple Service Request Types (Direct, Custom, Emergency, Broadcast)
✓ Real-time Location Tracking
✓ Secure In-app Payments
✓ Verified Mechanics & Shops
✓ Rating & Review System
✓ Document Verification
✓ 24/7 Emergency Service Availability
✓ Transparent Pricing
✓ Service History & Receipts
✓ Dispute Resolution Support

Our Commitment

MechConnect is committed to maintaining high standards of service quality and 
user safety. All mechanics and shops undergo verification processes, and we 
provide support for dispute resolution to ensure fair treatment for all users.
